## Task: Propose a Performance Design

The problem statement is in the file you have been given as `problem.md`.
Read it carefully before proceeding.

If context files were provided (listed in your briefing), read any that are relevant to your performance analysis before writing your proposal.

If this is round 2 or later, read your previous refinement from the file path given to you as your prior refinement. Use it as your starting point and advance it — do not start from scratch.

If clarifications were conducted, read the clarifications summary file provided in your briefing. The answers there are authoritative and must be reflected in your proposal.

---

Propose performance-related design for this problem. Focus on loads, bottlenecks, and constraints stated or implied by the problem. Skip or shorten areas the problem does not emphasize.

Use this structure:

## Performance Overview
Summarize the performance goals, expected load, and constraints.

## Key Bottlenecks & Risks
Identify likely sources of latency, contention, or inefficiency.

## Optimization Strategies
Outline approaches such as caching, load balancing, batching, or concurrency control.

## Resource Utilization Plan
Describe how to manage CPU, memory, storage, and network usage efficiently.

## Observability & Testing
Explain how performance will be measured and verified.

## Trade-offs & Justifications
Discuss trade-offs between performance, complexity, and maintainability.

You may add a final `## Requirements Coverage` section if needed to explicitly map requirements to your design (this section is also required by shared instructions).
